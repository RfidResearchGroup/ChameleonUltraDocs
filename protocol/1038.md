# 1038: GET_ALL_SLOT_NICKS

Get nickname of all slots HF/LF.

## Request Packet

```mermaid
---
title: "Request Packet"
---
packet
+8: "SOF"
+8: "LRC1"
+16: "CMD (=1038)"
+16: "STATUS"
+16: "LEN (=0)"
+8: "LRC2"
+8: "LRC3"
```

* DATA in Request Packet: None

## Response Packet

```mermaid
---
title: "Response Packet"
---
packet
+8: "SOF"
+8: "LRC1"
+16: "CMD (=1038)"
+16: "STATUS"
+16: "LEN (=0)"
+8: "LRC2"
+8: "len_hf_0"
+24: "name_hf_0 (len_hf_0 bytes)"
+8: "len_lf_0"
+24: "name_lf_0 (len_lf_0 bytes)"
+8: "len_hf_1"
+24: "name_hf_1 (len_hf_1 bytes)"
+8: "len_lf_1"
+24: "name_lf_1 (len_lf_1 bytes)"
+8: "len_hf_2"
+24: "name_hf_2 (len_hf_2 bytes)"
+8: "len_lf_2"
+24: "name_lf_2 (len_lf_2 bytes)"

+8: "len_lf_3"
+24: "name_lf_3 (len_lf_3 bytes)"
+8: "len_hf_4"
+24: "name_hf_4 (len_hf_4 bytes)"
+8: "len_lf_4"
+24: "name_lf_4 (len_lf_4 bytes)"

```

* DATA in Response Packet
    * For each slot (0-7):
        * len_hf_X: `1 byte`, length of HF nickname of slot X
        * name_hf_X: `len_hf_X bytes`, HF nickname of slot X (UTF-8)
        * len_lf_X: `1 byte`, length of LF nickname of slot X
        * name_lf_X: `len_lf_X bytes`, LF nickname of slot X (UTF-8)
