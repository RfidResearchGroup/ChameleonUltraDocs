# 4018: HF14A_GET_ANTI_COLL_DATA

Get anti-collision data from emulator.

* CLI: cf `hw slot list`/`hf mf econfig`/`hf mfu econfig`

## Request Packet

```mermaid
---
title: "Request Packet"
---
packet
+8: "SOF"
+8: "LRC1"
+16: "CMD (=4018)"
+16: "STATUS"
+16: "LEN (=0)"
+8: "LRC2"
+8: "LRC3"
```

* DATA in Request Packet: None

## Response Packet

```mermaid
---
title: "Response Packet"
---
packet
+8: "SOF"
+8: "LRC1"
+16: "CMD (=4018)"
+16: "STATUS"
+16: "LEN (=5+L1+L2)"
+8: "LRC2"
+8: "uidlen (=L1)"
+32: "uid (L1 bytes, L1=4, 7 or 10)"
+16: "atqa"
+8: "sak"
+8: "atslen (=L2)"
+32: "ats (L2 bytes, 0<=L2<=255)"
+8: "LRC3"
```

* DATA in Response Packet
    * uidlen: `1 byte`, length of UID (L1, L1 is 4, 7 or 10).
    * uid: `L1 bytes, L1=4, 7 or 10`, UID of the 14443-3 (Type A) tag.
    * atqa: `2 bytes`, ATQA of the 14443-3 (Type A) tag.
    * sak: `1 byte`, SAK of the 14443-3 (Type A) tag.
    * atslen: `1 byte`, length of ATS (L2, 0<=L2<=255).
    * ats: `L2 bytes, 0<=L2<=255`, ATS of the 14443-3 (Type A) tag.
